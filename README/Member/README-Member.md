## Members

Members are the primary users of the AHFUL app. Both gym and non-gym members fall under this account type, created to privately track your workout progress.

### Features available to Members:
- Log and track workouts with exercises, reps, and weights
- Record body measurements (weight, arm/leg circumference, etc.)
- View workout history and progress over time
- Explore nearby gyms that use the app
- Receive notifications and reminders for workouts
- Customizable home dashboard
- Food and nutrition logging
