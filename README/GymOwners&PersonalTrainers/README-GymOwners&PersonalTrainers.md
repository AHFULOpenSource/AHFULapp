## Gym Owners & Personal Trainers

### Gym Owners

Gym owners have the ability to create events and workout calendars, manage their gym's presence in the app, and attract new members through the built-in gym discovery features.

### Personal Trainers

Personal trainers can manage clients, create and assign workouts, schedule exercises with specific reps and weights, and edit workout calendars for their clients.

### Features available to Gym Owners & Personal Trainers:
- Create and manage gym profiles and events
- Assign workouts and exercise plans to clients
- Schedule workout calendars
- Track client progress
- Promote your business within the app
