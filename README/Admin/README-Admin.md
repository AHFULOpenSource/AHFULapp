## Admins

Admins have access to monitor member accounts and enforce the application's terms of service. They are responsible for keeping the community safe and ensuring the app runs smoothly.

### Features available to Admins:
- Monitor member accounts and activity
- Ban or deactivate accounts violating terms of service
- Review reported content and users
- Manage application settings and configurations
